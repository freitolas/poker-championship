# Grand Finale: Closing Ceremony Script (funny titles)

**How to use this script**
- Lines in quote blocks (`>`) are read aloud.
- Lines marked *🔒 Host notes* are just for you. Don't read them out, or you'll give away the winner.
- Each award builds up with clues, and the name only comes at the very end.
- The three open titles have a short block for each likely winner. On the night, read only the block for whoever actually won.

Numbers come from the results sheet after 14 games in 2026, using the same rules as the app's Titles tab.

---
